Cluster signals across both strategies

The two strategies run the same 5/50-MA + cushion signal on different price series: Defensive on the cluster's absolute PC1 price; Aggressive on the cluster's relative-strength line (cluster return minus market return, compounded). The columns mean:

  • Defensive State: LONG when the signal on the cluster's absolute price is in its bullish phase, FLAT otherwise.
  • Aggressive State: LEAD = signal LONG on the cluster's RS-vs-market line (cluster has been outperforming the market by the signal's reckoning); LAG = signal FLAT on that line. So LEAD/LAG is LONG/FLAT on a relative price series, not a real-time outperformance ranking.
  • Days: calendar days since that signal entered its current state. Includes the 30-day min-hold floor and the volatility cushion, so it can lag raw price moves by a few weeks — that's by design, it's exactly how the strategy would treat the cluster.
  • Perf: cluster PC1 absolute cum return from the state-entry date to today — what the cluster actually did in price terms during the current state, for both columns.
  • Today: a badge appears when the state flipped on the most recent bar (highlighted row).
  • Imminent: probability of imminent crossover (h=5), shown only when > 0.8.
  • Basket (under each theme name): the 1–5 ETFs the Defensive Strategy uses to replicate the cluster (NNLS-fit on cluster PC1). These are what you'd actually trade to express the cluster.
